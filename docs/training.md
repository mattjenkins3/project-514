# The 12-week training approach

The main climbing goal is to keep movement precise and efficient through the final third of a hard route. Running builds toward a sustainable 10-mile week. These are ceilings that need to fit your recent activity and recovery. A stronger project attempt or steadier final-third movement is useful progress even without a new grade.

| Week | Climbing ceiling | Running ceiling | Focus |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 150 min | 6 mi | Easy reentry and movement quality |
| 2 | 180 min | 7 mi | Relaxed grip and economical movement |
| 3 | 215 min | 8 mi | Pacing and rests |
| 4 | 150 min | 6 mi | Deload and record a repeatable benchmark |
| 5 | 240 min | 9 mi | Carefully introduce route pairs |
| 6 | 240 min | 10 mi | Same climbing ceiling; optional controlled run intervals with clearance |
| 7 | 270 min | 10 mi | Keep efforts repeatable |
| 8 | 180 min | 7 mi | Deload and compare a matched benchmark |
| 9 | 270 min | 10 mi | Route pairs and planning the last crux |
| 10 | 270 min | 10 mi | Project attempts with full recovery |
| 11 | 270 min | 10 mi | Return to route pairs with clean technique |
| 12 | 180 min | 8 mi | Taper and use a rested assessment if appropriate |

The default rhythm is technique or controlled bouldering Monday, an easy run and optional strength Tuesday, low-pump climbing Wednesday, running Thursday, full rest Friday, route work Saturday, and an easy run with a short review Sunday. Fridays stay off. Unfavorable or missing feedback can shorten, soften, or remove a session.

The coach never adds work to make up a missed day. It avoids hard finger sessions closer than 48 hours, automatic fingerboard work, and intense running without clearance. Stop painful activity. Significant or worsening symptoms require review before a return. New or concerning chest or breathing symptoms warrant urgent medical help.

Fueling supports weight stability and recovery. Energy and macro figures are provisional reference points. The core habits are regular meals, carbohydrate around demanding sessions, protein across the day, and fluids.
